How fast is my ERP ready after I sign up?
Once payment is confirmed, Volqen creates your own ERP site automatically and emails your login. The target is minutes, not weeks; exact timing will be confirmed when the service launches.
Is my data separate from other customers?
Yes. Every customer gets a separate ERP site with its own private database. Your data is never mixed with another company's.
Which countries and currencies do you support?
Volqen runs on ERPNext, which supports multiple currencies, multiple companies and regional tax setups for many countries. You choose your country and base currency at sign-up.
What is Volqen built on?
Volqen is powered by the open-source Frappe / ERPNext platform, used by thousands of companies worldwide. Volqen packages it with industry setups, tier-based permissions, hosting, onboarding and support.
Can I change plans later?
Plan changes are part of the service design. Upgrades unlock additional modules and users on your existing site without moving your data.
