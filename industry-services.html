ERP for Professional Services

Bill every hour, track every project, collect every invoice.

Get startedBook a demo

Overview

Service businesses sell time and expertise. Volqen is designed to link contracts, timesheets, project cost and invoicing, including provincial sales tax on services.

KPIs to track

  • Billable utilisation %
  • Revenue per employee
  • Project margin
  • Days sales outstanding
Typical challenges

What holds professional services businesses back.

Unbilled hours

Work is delivered but never invoiced.

Unclear project margins

Staff cost is not allocated to clients and projects.

Provincial service tax complexity

Different provinces apply different sales tax rules on services.

ERP workflows

How Volqen is designed to run the work.

Core workflows configured for professional services.

  1. Contracts & retainers

    Recurring invoices for retainers and service contracts.

  2. Timesheets

    Time recorded per client, project and task.

  3. Project billing

    Milestone and time-based invoicing.

  4. Collections

    Ageing and follow-up for receivables.

Business benefits

What you can expect to improve.

Results depend on your processes, data quality and adoption. These are the areas an ERP is designed to strengthen.

  • Higher billable utilisation
  • Margin by client and project
  • Fewer missed invoices
  • Clear tax registers per province
